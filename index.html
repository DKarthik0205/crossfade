Where do I get a Client ID?
Crossfade runs entirely in your browser, so it needs its own Spotify app registration — free and takes about a minute.
  1. Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard and log in.
  2. Click Create app. Name it anything, e.g. "Crossfade".
  3. For Redirect URI, paste exactly this page's URL:
  4. Enable the Web API checkbox, save, then open the app's Settings and copy the Client ID into the field above.

Build your Apple Music list
No Apple account or API key needed. Crossfade pulls every track from the playlist and gives each one a direct Apple Music search link — tap it, then tap the + in Apple Music to add it to your library or a new playlist there.
Runs entirely in your browser. Your Spotify credentials are never sent anywhere but Spotify's own servers, and nothing is ever sent to Apple — Crossfade only builds search links you open yourself.
